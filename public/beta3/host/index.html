If it goes wrong

"I can't join, it says I'm taking a break"

Only the owner clears it: Owner Console › Play Online › Play Online › Cooldowns › Clear on their row, write a note, Clear. The moderator cannot. The first 2 missed READY taps a day are free; the break starts at the 3rd.

"My game looks old / no role reveal / no Game Night card"

Close the app fully, open it, wait 20 seconds, close it, open it again. Settings must read Nov 23 or later. Still wrong: once more on Wi-Fi.

"I've been waiting and no table"

Stay on the waiting screen: a table needs 8, or 4 after 3 minutes. The 7:50 PM second call-up covers missing seat holders. Never ask players to cancel and re-tap.

"I can't install / can't sign in"

iPhone: the public TestFlight link works for anyone (https://testflight.apple.com/join/dBMZkzhf). PC or browser: play at play.spysocial.app (sign in or play as a guest, then Settings › Birth Year › I Agree); no list. Android: the sign-in form closed at Tue noon; send the name to the agent; last Android adds by 6:30 PM, later than that, iPhone or browser. Questions go to Discord.

"I got no Game Night Intel" / "I can't buy an Intercept"

Ask which game and their @username; tell the agent. A loss, a game with no winner, leaving early or idling pays 0 by design. A win pays 500 if they played at least half of the turns. Tier 1 needs 400.

"I could see someone's role" / tables stuck on the role card

Ask for a screenshot in #beta-3-bugs and tell the agent. Ask a second phone to check. Stuck tables: everyone at that table closes fully and reopens.

"The mic does not work" / "where is voice chat?"

There is no voice chat in the app. The mic turns speech into text. Talk in the Discord voice room.

Raelys sends something to you

Alert the Owner rings your phone as an urgent report: open it from the Owner Console home (open reports). Her Needs the Owner and Ask the Owner (a stuck table) do not ring: they show on the home as moderators waiting, and she also posts one line in #mod-chat.

A crash on 2+ phones, or anything else

Tell the agent (what, which table, how many phones). It ships a fix between games when it can; nothing is switched off.

Running late

The 8:00 call and the 9:00 thank-you happen whatever is running. Games already dealt finish on their own.

Switch the new role reveal off? Only the owner decides. Off = the old role card for every game, games already running too (each phone within about 5 minutes; at once after close and reopen).

Only if (1) a Local can see who the spies are, or a spy's phone shows the place, and a second phone shows the same; or (2) 2 or more tables cannot get past the reveal even after close and reopen.

Anything else: leave it on. To switch off, tell the agent kill switch, then say on voice: "Close the app fully and open it again."
