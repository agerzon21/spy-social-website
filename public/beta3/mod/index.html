Mod Console

What you do in the app

  1. Open the Mod Console

    On your phone, signed in as yourself: Account › Mod Console. It does not open in a browser. Accept the moderator agreement if it asks. Before Tuesday 6 PM: open the app once, allow notifications and check the Console opens (you will see Reports and, under Also waiting, Game Night; Quick Review shows only while some reports can be closed from a list). If a Safety hold alerts card shows, tap Turn On.

  2. Reports

    Open Reports on the Console home every 5 minutes or so and take them from the top: the list is already in the owner's order (most serious first, then oldest); a red "Over 24 hours" tag counts the late ones. Read the chat around the report; other languages translate by themselves (tap Translate Every Line if one is left). You never see drawings of sensitive cases: if the chat does not settle a drawing report, tap Decide › Needs the Owner.

  3. Decide a report

    Open the report. Dismiss (nothing to act on) sits next to Decide. Decide lists Warn (a warning on their record), Mute chat (1 hour, 1 day or 7 days), Pause from Play Online (1, 3 or 7 days), Pause and Propose Ban (severe), Propose a ban (only the owner bans) and Needs the Owner (unsure). Pick the reason the player sees, then write your note for the owner (10 characters or more; the player never sees it or your name). Warn first, then mute.

  4. Remove a chat line

    On the reported player's own lines (tagged This player) tap Remove Line. In a room you are in, or at a Game Night table you Watch, tap a player for their sheet: Kick and Open a Case at Play Online and Game Night tables (both public); in a private room only Open a Case.

  5. Child safety and crisis

    Never decide these. On the case (or a player's sheet) tap Alert the Owner, pick the kind and write a short note: his phone gets an urgent push and the case leaves your queue. Do not investigate or discuss it. Safety holds on the Console home are such cases the owner has not opened for 15 minutes: you may only Hold one (pause 24 hours, remove from the room, or hide their lines) with a reason; never decide it.

  6. Game Night page

    Mod Console › Game Night shows the live tables (a stuck one shows first). You can Watch a table read-only. For a stuck or broken table tap Ask the Owner. Ask the Owner and Needs the Owner do not ring his phone: also post one line in #mod-chat. A table with someone under 18 or without a birth year is the owner's to watch. You never start or end the night.

Alert the Owner

Ping the owner

Use Alert the Owner in the Mod Console (on the case, or on a player's sheet in a room) only for:

  • Anything about minors or someone who seems under 18
  • Child safety of any kind, a child at risk, self-harm or someone in crisis, a threat of violence, sexual content

On a case, use Decide instead for:

  • Bans or ban requests: Propose a ban (you may only propose)
  • Anything you are unsure about: Needs the Owner

Payments, refunds, payouts, a tester on a break, a bug that blocks the game, anything on Discord, and everything else: say it in #mod-chat. The owner is hosting voice, so keep it short.

You may

  • Read and handle reports in the Mod Console (Reports, Quick Review)
  • Remove chat lines (Remove Line)
  • Warn, mute, pause up to 7 days, and kick at Play Online and Game Night tables (not private rooms)
  • Watch Game Night tables and help in #beta-3-ask-hq; delete spam and time out strangers who spam #beta-3-ask-hq or #beta-3-bugs (every Beta 3 channel is open to everyone tonight)
  • Fair Play and community pack review

You may not

  • Permanent bans (propose only; the owner confirms)
  • Clear a "taking a break" for a tester (only the owner can: post their @username in #mod-chat)
  • Items, Intel, economy, minting, switches, payments or payouts
  • Personal details of anyone: never share names, chats, drawings or case details outside the Console, and never screenshot reports
  • Start or end Game Night
  • Act on friends or on your own reports: tell the owner. Unsure or the owner is unreachable: leave the case open, do not improvise
