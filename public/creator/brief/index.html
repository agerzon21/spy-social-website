3 · Host · Spy SketchStart your room, invite your crew

A few clicks from Home. Every rule starts at its recommended value: leave them as they are.

  • Your crew opens your link (or Private Room › Join a Friend's Room › code › Join), then Ready Up! 3 players minimum. With 4 or more: Use Recommended, then Dismiss.
  • Next game: Game Over › Back to Room (foot of the right panel) › Start Game.
  • The room code shows at the top of the lobby: cut or cover it in the edit.

4 · Record your side (everyone)

The Short's frame: the centre panel under your face cam.

  • 1Chrome, Creator Mode, F11. Everyone on their own computer, Chrome zoom 100% (Ctrl+0).
  • 2Discord voice on headphones. Don't share your screen: your card is on it.
  • 3OBS: canvas 1920 × 1080. Crop Display Capture to the centre panel: the OBS CROP numbers are at the foot of the left panel (1920 × 1080 screen: Left 528 · Right 528 · Top 28 · Bottom 27). Webcam beside it, never over the game.
  • 4Sync: everyone presses record, you count "3, 2, 1", everyone claps once. Record the whole session; each player sends their file to your editor.

If something's off

  • Stay in Creator Mode while recording (left by accident? Settings › Recording › Creator Mode).
  • “Reconnecting…” shows in the side panel, not the recording: wait, or press Back Home on the card if it stays. A frozen screen with no card: reload.
  • In chat, no emoji picker or Chinese, Japanese or Korean keyboard (its pop-up can cover the game).

The videoPlay it like you always do

5 · The videoJust play. Like always.

  • Play a normal round or two of Spy Sketch, exactly like your other drawing-imposter Shorts. Same crew, same energy, same edit.
  • Be yourselves. The reveals, the terrible drawings, the accusations and the vote are the video.
  • No feature tour. Don't explain items, Uniques, memberships or menus. Nobody sits through that, and our website covers it.
  • Viewers can be under 18: don't open Intercepts on camera or call them loot, crates or boxes, and don't say "win", "chance", "odds", "rare" or "unlock" about items. Ranked and tournaments are "coming": no prizes or money talk.

6 · The endingOne line. 10 seconds max.

The idea: same game you've been playing forever, now on a cooler platform that looks better, and viewers can come play it themselves. A and B take about 8 seconds, C about 5. Pick one, or say it your way:

A

“Want to play like us? SpySocial, free to download on the App Store and Google Play, use code LEVVIS. Ranked and tournaments coming.”

B

“Same game we've played forever, way cooler now. SpySocial, free to download, use code LEVVIS. Ranked and tournaments coming… you know the vibe.”

C

“SpySocial. Free to download, use code LEVVIS. Ranked and tournaments coming.”

  • Free to download on iPhone and Android: spysocial.app (worth putting on screen during the line). Say "free to download", not "free forever"; membership is optional. Ranked and tournaments: "coming", no dates.
  • Don't name or show other games, their logos or ranks in the ending, the title, the description or the thumbnail. A wink is fine. Store badges: only Apple's and Google's official badge images, unaltered; saying the names is fine.
