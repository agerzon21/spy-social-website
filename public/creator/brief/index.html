A free party game of social deception. In Spy Sketch everyone adds to one drawing of a secret word, except the spy, who bluffs along. You play with friends in your own private room, in your browser. Anything this guide doesn't cover: message us on Discord (page 5).

1 · Sign in, once before the day
  • 1Open play.spysocial.app in Chrome › I Have an Account, even the first time.
  • 2Type the email your manager sent us, then Continue with Email. Copy the 6-digit code from your inbox and click Paste Code. No password.
  • 3Click Next (or Skip), then Let's Play: @levvis is already reserved for you, nothing to change.
  • 4Home asks your Birth Year: type it (18 or older), Yes, That's Right, then I Agree to the rules. Your account switches on: Lifetime membership, browser play and Creator Mode.
  • 5Click through the one-time pop-ups once (the Home tour: Next, then Got It, or Skip; Drawings Are Checked, the rules; Skip a New to Spy Games? sheet), so they don't pop up while you record.

Your co-players do the same with the emails we have for them, or log in from your room link: birth year at the room's door, then straight into your lobby in Creator Mode. Another email gets a plain account without the set-up, so tell us first.

2 · Creator Mode: what gets recorded

Good to know

  • Creator Mode opens by itself after your birth year. If the Home tour opens, click Next / Got It (or Skip). If it ever doesn't: your agent (top right of Home) › the gear › Recording › Creator Mode.
  • For the cleanest recording, press F11 (full screen; recommended, not required) — the OBS crop numbers on the left are for full screen. Prefer a window? Use OBS Window Capture and crop out the panel on the left yourself.
  • The panels at the sides, in games AND menus (Home, Private Room, Settings...), are for you only (labelled ONLY YOU SEE THIS) and are not recorded: the recorded frame is the middle box. The screen title and back arrow are inside the box and are recorded.
  • Your card sits face down in the right panel: click to see your word or role, click again to hide it. It never shows in the recording.
  • Same Sound for Every Role is already on, so nobody on the call can hear your role.
  • Before your first recording, play the Spy Sketch tutorial: Home › How to Play › Spy Sketch › Tutorial › As a Local. It is a short guided intro on a practice table in one panel (no side columns), so it looks different from a real game; the secret word stays off camera. End Tutorial › Leave quits any time; the back arrow brings you to Home, still in Creator Mode.

4 · Play · what you'll see and recordDraw it. Or fake it.

How each side wins

  • Locals: vote out the spy before time's up. When the clock runs out, everyone gets one Final Vote.
  • The spy: survive it, or guess the word (Solve, from round 2). Right, the spy wins; wrong, the spy's out.
  • Sketch break: everyone votes Keep this page or New page.
  • Game Over shows the result in the centre panel: click PAGE 1, 2… to jump to that page's last round. Chat is always open in the left panel.

Drawing with a mouse

  • The mouse draws where it points; a ring shows your line's width.
  • The mouse wheel changes the width.
  • Hold Shift for a straight line (or a perfect circle or square with Shapes).
  • Hold the right button to rub out your own lines from this turn.
  • Steady Hand (5th tool button, off until you click it): your line follows the mouse smoothly.

5 · Recording setup · everyone does thisRecord your side

Your Short's frame: the centre panel (1080 × 1281) under a 639 px face cam, from the lobby to Game Over.

  • 1Chrome, Creator Mode (F11 recommended). Everyone on their own computer.
  • 2Discord. One voice channel, on headphones. Don't share your screen: your card is on it. Overlay and notifications off.
  • 3OBS, once. Settings › Video: base and output 1920 × 1080, 60 fps (30 is fine). Chrome zoom at 100% (Ctrl+0). Crop the centre panel and put the webcam beside it, never over the game. The numbers are for full screen; in a window, use Window Capture and crop out both side columns yourself (the OBS crop numbers are shown in the left column):

  • 4Test. Do Not Disturb on; record 10 seconds and play it back with sound.
  • 5Sync. Everyone presses record, the host counts "3, 2, 1" and everyone claps once. Record the whole session and send your file to the editor.
For the editor
  • One file per player, synced on the clap.
  • The cropped panel (864 × 1025) scaled × 1.25 is 1080 × 1281: it fills the game area under a 639 px face cam, nothing to cut again.
  • Cut to the drawer while drawing, the accused at the vote, the spy at the reveal.

Watch for

  • Keep the SpySocial name on screen: a small tag in a corner for the first seconds and at the end, plus play.spysocial.app (logo files in the press kit).
  • Stay in Creator Mode the whole time: leaving changes the crop. Exit Creator Mode (under Full Screen) leaves in one click. To leave Full Screen, press Esc (on a Mac you can also use Fn+F). Left by accident? Settings › Recording › Creator Mode turns it back on.
  • Typing in chat: no emoji picker or Chinese, Japanese or Korean keyboard (the system pop-up can cover the game). Anything odd in the recorded box: pause and tell us.
  • Keep the connection steady: use a wired connection (or sit near the router) and keep the game tab visible, in front. If “Reconnecting…” shows, wait for it to clear and cut that part. Don’t reload.
  • A screen sticks? Reload: the game comes back in a second or two, and F11 keeps you in full screen.
