4

Set up to record

Everyone, own computer
  • Chrome at 100% zoom (Ctrl+0), then F11 for full screen.
  • Discord voice on headphones. Don't share your screen: your card is on it.
  • Everyone presses record, you count "3, 2, 1", everyone claps once. Each player sends their file to your editor.

OBS, canvas 1920 × 1080: crop Display Capture with the numbers under OBS CROP (left side), so only the yellow frame is captured. Your numbers depend on your screen. Webcam beside the game, never over it.

Once everyone has seen their role, play like you always do. Unmute whenever you want: your call.
