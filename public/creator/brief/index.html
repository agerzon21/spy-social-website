2 · On recording day: open your room

  • 2The lobby opens on Spy Sketch. Leave the rules as they are. If a card about drawing checks shows, click GOT IT. If the lobby tour shows, click SKIP.

The room code shows at the top of the lobby: cut or cover it in the edit.

3 · Recording set-up (everyone, on their own computer)
  • 1Chrome at 100% zoom (Ctrl+0), then F11 for full screen.
  • 2Discord voice on headphones. Don't share your screen: your card is on it.
  • 3OBS, canvas 1920 × 1080: crop Display Capture with the numbers under OBS CROP (left side of the screen), so only the centre panel (IN THE RECORDING) is captured. Webcam beside the game, never over it.
  • 4Everyone presses record, you count "3, 2, 1", everyone claps once. Each player sends their file to your editor.
