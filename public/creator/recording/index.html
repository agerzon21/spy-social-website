If something goes wrong while recording

Tue Nov 24. Keep this open beside OBS. Set-up and the game are in your Creator Brief; this page is only for fixes. Most fixes: pause, fix, record that part again.

  1. 1
    Something private showed in the centre panel (a role, the word, the spies' chat)Stop recording and tell us. Record that part again; don't count on cutting it later. Private things belong in the side panels (tagged Only you see this), never in the centre panel framed in yellow.
  2. 2
    A player drops outPause. He reloads the page, or opens play.spysocial.app and clicks Rejoin on the Private Room card. A turn he misses may be skipped.
  3. 3
    The game seems stuckReload first. Still stuck: the host clicks Leave › End Game for Everyone (everyone goes back to the lobby), then Start Game. Anyone else can pick Vote to End the Game.
  4. 4
    The OBS crop numbers are missing or look wrongRead them on Home, in the lobby or a menu; they hide during a game and after the page's own Full Screen button (press Esc, then F11). No numbers, only "Before you record: browser zoom 100%…"? Set Chrome zoom to 100% (Ctrl+0) and close Chrome's side panel.
  5. 5
    "Make the window wider to record" or "Use a landscape screen to record"The window is snapped or too narrow, or the screen stands upright. Widen the window (or use a landscape screen), press F11 and read the numbers again.
  6. 6
    Someone left Creator ModePause. Turn it back on (Settings › Recording › Creator Mode), read the crop numbers again and record that part again: while it was off, the crop didn't match and private things could have been in frame.
  7. 7
    The sign-in code didn't arriveCheck spam and use the exact email we have for you. Wait a minute, then click Resend Code once.
  8. 8
    A co-player has no Creator ModeCreator Mode switches on only for the invited email. Browser play itself is open to everyone, so he can play; send us the email he logged in with if he needs it.
  9. 9
    Echo or double sound on the callSomeone isn't on headphones. Game music is off in a browser by default, so the echo is almost always the call.
  10. 10
    Practice and How to Play are not for recordingThe Spy Sketch tutorial now looks like the real game (your word stays in the side panels), but it is practice. Play it before the day; record real games only.
