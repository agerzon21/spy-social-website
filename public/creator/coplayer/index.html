SpySocial in two lines

A free party game of social deception. In Spy Sketch everyone adds to one drawing of a secret word, except the spy, who has to fake it.

You're playing in Levvis's Short, a SpySocial partnership video. This page gets you into his room; page 2 is how to play and record. Questions? Message us on Discord (page 2).

Sign in, once before the day
  • 1On a computer, open play.spysocial.app in Chrome, click I Have an Account (even the first time).
  • 2Type the email Levvis's team sent us, then Continue with Email. Copy the 6-digit code from your inbox and click Paste Code. No password.
  • 3Click Next (or Skip), then Let's Play: your creator name is already reserved.
  • 4Home asks your Birth Year: type it (18 or older), Yes, That's Right, then I Agree to the rules. Your name, Lifetime membership, browser play and Creator Mode switch on.
  • 5Click through the one-time pop-ups once (Drawings Are Checked, the rules; Skip a New to Spy Games? sheet) before you record.

Browser play is open to everyone. Creator Mode switches on only for the email we have on file, so sign in with that one.

How to play · what you'll see and recordDraw it. Or fake it.

  • AClick your card: Locals see the secret word, the spy gets a dark Spy card. During the game it sits face down in the right panel: click to see it, click again to hide it.
  • BTake turns adding to one shared drawing, then Finish Drawing. With a mouse: the wheel changes the line's size, Shift draws a straight line, the right button rubs out your lines from this turn, Steady Hand (5th tool) smooths them. Spy? Draw something vague that fits.
  • CActions › Vote (right panel) calls a vote. Catch the spy and the Locals win; time's up means one Final Vote. From round 2 the spy can Solve: one guess at the word.
  • DAfter Game Over: Back to Room (foot of the right panel), then Ready Up! if you're not still ready.

Record your side

  • 1Creator Mode + F11. It opens by itself after your birth year (else: your agent › the gear › Recording › Creator Mode). For the cleanest recording, press F11 for full screen (recommended, not required). To leave Full Screen, press Esc (on a Mac you can also use Fn+F). The OBS crop numbers are for full screen. Prefer a window? Use OBS Window Capture and crop out the panel on the left yourself. The panels at the sides are for you only (labelled ONLY YOU SEE THIS) and are not recorded: the recorded frame is the middle box.
  • 2Discord. Join the voice channel, on headphones. Don't share your screen: your card is on it. Overlay and notifications off.
  • 3OBS, once. Video: base and output 1920 × 1080, Chrome zoom 100% (Ctrl+0). Display Capture cropped to the centre panel (1920 × 1080 screen: Left 528 · Right 528 · Top 28 · Bottom 27; other screens: the OBS CROP numbers left of the panel) at 0, 0, size 864 × 1025. Webcam at 864, 0, size 1056 × 1080, beside the game, never over it.
  • 4Test 10 seconds with sound, Do Not Disturb on. Sync: everyone presses record, Levvis counts "3, 2, 1", everyone claps once. Record the whole session; send your file to the editor.
  • 5While recording: stay in Creator Mode (left by accident? Settings › Recording › Creator Mode). In chat, no emoji picker or Chinese, Japanese or Korean keyboard. Anything odd in the recorded box: pause and tell Levvis.
