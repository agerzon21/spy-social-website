For anyone playing with LevvisYou're in the Short

You're playing Spy Sketch in Levvis's Short, a SpySocial partnership video, in his private room at play.spysocial.app, on a computer. Questions? Discord: discord.gg/B8MG3xrV6f (or DM Alex there).

1 · Sign in once, by Fri Nov 27
  • 1On your computer, open play.spysocial.app in Chrome. Click I Have an Account (not Play Now).
  • 2In Email, type the email address Levvis's team gave us for you, then click Continue with Email.
  • 3Copy the 6-digit code from our email, then click Paste Code.
  • 4Set Up Your Profile: your name is already filled in. Click Next, then Let's Play.
  • 5What Year Were You Born? Type your birth year › Continue › Yes, That's Right › I Agree.
  • 6If New to Spy Games? shows, click I Know How to Play. If the Home tour shows, click Skip.

Creator Mode then turns on by itself.

2 · On recording day
  • 1Open the invite link Levvis sends you. Or, on Home: Private Room › Join a Friend's Room › type his code under Have a Room Code? › Join.
  • 2If the Drawings Are Checked card shows, click Got It. Then click Ready Up!
  • 3Chrome at 100% zoom (Ctrl+0), then F11 for full screen. Discord voice on headphones. Don't share your screen: your card is on it.
  • 4OBS, canvas 1920 × 1080: crop Display Capture with the numbers under OBS crop (left side of the screen), so only the centre panel (In the recording) is captured. Webcam beside the game, never over it.
  • 5Everyone presses record, Levvis counts "3, 2, 1", everyone claps once. Send your file to Levvis's editor.

Before Levvis presses Start Game, mute your mic: each role plays a different sound. Unmute once everyone has seen their role.

3 · Play
  • 1Play a normal round or two, like your usual drawing-imposter games. No feature tour: Levvis says the one closing line about SpySocial. Don't open Intercepts on camera.
  • 2Next game: on Game Over, click Back to Room.
  • 3If you ever mention SpySocial on your own channel or socials, say it gave you free items.
